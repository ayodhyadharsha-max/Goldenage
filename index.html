MARKET INTEGRITY

Gurgaon Luxury Housing & Dwarka Expressway FAQ

Direct answers to the most common questions searched by voice queries and AI search systems.

The 29-km long Dwarka Expressway offers direct signal-free connectivity between Delhi (Dwarka) and Gurgaon (NH-48). High-ticket investors prefer this zone because of its proximity to the Indira Gandhi International (IGI) Airport, the newly planned International Exhibition-Convention Centre (IACC), and the upcoming Global City project. Property values along this corridor have seen robust capital appreciation of over 12% CAGR, positioning it as Gurgaon’s top premium residential investment hub.

Luxury residential projects along Dwarka Expressway and New Gurgaon primarily offer spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK layouts, along with duplexes and ultra-premium penthouses. A standard luxury 3 BHK starts from 2,300 sq.ft. and can go up to 3,000 sq.ft., while 4 BHK apartments range between 3,200 sq.ft. and 4,500 sq.ft. They feature dedicated servant suites, modular kitchens, grand wraps-around balconies, and private foyers.

Most developers offering high-ticket apartments in Sector 106, Sector 36A, and surrounding areas offer flexible, buyer-friendly payment models. These include:

  • Construction Linked Plans (CLP): Payment spread across structural construction milestones.
  • Possession Linked Plans (PLP): Commonly structured as 30:70 or 40:60, where the bulk of the payment is settled upon key handover.
  • Time-Linked Custom Plans: Installments divided over a set time block (e.g. 10% booking, 15% every 6 months).
